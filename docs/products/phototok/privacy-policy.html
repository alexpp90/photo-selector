Privacy Policy — PhotoTok

Last updated: October 2026 · Deutsche Version unten

1. Summary

PhotoTok (Android package com.phototok) is a free, open-source, 100% on-device photo culling application. Your photos, EXIF metadata, and usage data never leave your device. PhotoTok requests zero runtime permissions, contains no analytics, no advertising SDKs, no crash-reporting telemetry, and no cloud backend.

2. Controller (Art. 4(7) GDPR)

Alexander Patz
Contact: see Legal Notice (Impressum) or via GitHub at github.com/alexpp90/photo-selector.

3. Data Processed by the Application

  • Photos and EXIF Metadata (Local Only): You select folders explicitly using the Android System Storage Access Framework (SAF) folder picker (ACTION_OPEN_DOCUMENT_TREE). PhotoTok reads image headers, EXIF tags, and thumbnails locally in device memory to display photos and perform file operations (Copy, Move to Selection, or Delete/Trash) inside the folder you selected, as well as optional system-confirmed MediaStore favourite and recycle-bin requests on Android 11+. When you explicitly invoke Share or Open with…, Android's system chooser (Intent.createChooser) grants temporary read access solely for the selected photo(s) to the target app you choose. No images, thumbnails, file names, GPS coordinates, or EXIF tags are ever transmitted over the network.
  • App Preferences, Seen-Photo Fingerprints & Local Statistics (App-Private Storage): User settings (such as swipe filing action, custom folder names, sort order, local favourites, pending-delete recovery journals, session/lifetime culling counters, and persisted SAF folder URI grants) are stored locally in the app's private Android Jetpack DataStore sandbox, alongside an on-device log of 64-bit seen-photo fingerprints (seen_photos.log). Cloud auto-backup of app data is disabled (android:allowBackup="false") so local paths, fingerprints, and statistics are never uploaded to cloud backups.
  • No Network / No Third-Party SDKs: PhotoTok does not request the android.permission.INTERNET permission in production for telemetry or analytics and integrates no third-party tracking, profiling, or crash-reporting services.

4. Legal Basis (Art. 6 GDPR)

Because all processing takes place strictly locally on your device at your initiative and no personal data is transmitted to the developer or any third party, no external processing of personal data occurs. To the extent local device storage is accessed, this happens solely to provide the functionality explicitly requested by you (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR / § 25(2) No. 2 TDDDG).

5. Data Retention & Deletion

All preferences remain on your device until you clear the application storage in Android Settings or uninstall PhotoTok. Uninstalling the app immediately deletes all locally stored preferences and revokes all SAF folder access grants.

6. Your Rights under GDPR

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (Arts. 15–21 GDPR), you have the right to request information, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability, and to object to processing, as well as the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR). Because the developer holds no personal data from your use of PhotoTok, you exercise full control over all app data directly on your device.

3. Verarbeitete Daten auf dem Endgerät

  • Fotos und EXIF-Metadaten (ausschließlich lokal): Der Zugriff auf Fotoordner erfolgt ausschließlich über die vom Nutzer explizit im Android Storage Access Framework (SAF, ACTION_OPEN_DOCUMENT_TREE) ausgewählten Verzeichnisse. Die Auswertung von Bilddaten, EXIF-Informationen, vom System bestätigte MediaStore-Favoriten-/Papierkorb-Aktionen (ab Android 11) sowie Dateioperationen (Kopieren, Verschieben in den Auswahl-Ordner oder Löschen) finden vollständig lokal auf Ihrem Gerät statt. Nutzen Sie aktiv die Funktion Teilen oder Öffnen mit…, übergibt der Android-Systemdialog (Intent.createChooser) die ausgewählte Datei ausschließlich an die von Ihnen gewählte Ziel-App. Es erfolgt keinerlei Übertragung an den Entwickler oder unbeteiligte Dritte.
  • App-Einstellungen, Gesehen-Status & lokale Statistiken (privater App-Speicher): Einstellungen (z. B. Wisch-Aktion, Zielordnername, Sortierung, lokale Favoriten, Lösch-Journale, lokale Sichtungsstatistiken und gespeicherte Ordner-URIs) sowie lokale 64-Bit-Prüfsummen bereits gesichteter Fotos (seen_photos.log) werden ausschließlich lokal im privaten App-Speicher abgelegt. Ein automatischer Cloud-Backup-Export ist deaktiviert (android:allowBackup="false").
